# What's new in Kiln 1.10

Kiln 1.10 was released on 2026-11-15. Below: what it adds, what works
differently, what to do before you upgrade, and what is still broken. Every
change, one line each, is in the [1.10.0 changelog](../changelog/v1.10.0.md).

## At a glance

- **Site admins** can require two-step sign-in of their admins, their editors
  or everyone, with a grace period, and see who is covered and who is held.
- **Platform admins** can schedule backups inside Kiln, daily to monthly at a
  local time, and keep each schedule's newest N backups however old.
- **Admins** can limit which records a reference field accepts, and let
  editors create a missing record straight from the field.
- **Site admins** can send security alerts to a Slack (or Mattermost,
  Rocket.Chat, Discord) channel, and get alerted when the block list refuses
  a request.
- **Everyone with an account** gets stronger second factors: a passkey works
  at the code prompt, adding a factor emails the owner, removing a passkey
  asks for a second factor, and authenticator secrets are stored encrypted.

## Before you upgrade

Read the [Upgrade notes and Breaking sections](../changelog/v1.10.0.md) in
full; `mix kiln.update` prints them, and the known issues, before it moves
your pin. In short:

1. **Run `mix ash.migrate`.** It adds the tables `backup_schedules`,
   `platform_security_alerts` and `site_second_factor_policy`, and nullable
   columns on `webhook_endpoints`, `notifications`, `backup_runs` and `users`.
   None of them rewrites a table. About ten minutes after boot, a job moves
   authenticator-app secrets into the new encrypted columns. Rolling back
   after that job ran needs the step in the
   [TOTP upgrade note](../changelog/v1.10.0.md#totp-secrets-encrypted-upgrade).
2. **Replace `scripts/backup.sh` on every host where cron runs it.** An older
   copy can record a quiet live run as Interrupted, and deletes the new
   `kiln-<stamp>.hold` markers a schedule's keep settings rely on. It now also
   refuses a `BACKUP_KEEP_DAYS` that is not a whole number of days.
3. **Allow the new scheduled egress, or turn it off.** One node fetches
   Google's, Bing's, Apple's and DuckDuckGo's crawler IP lists every 6 hours.
   Allow those hosts, or set `KILN_CRAWLER_RANGES=false`. See
   [System requirements](../system-requirements.md#outbound-calls-made-by-default).
4. **Before rolling back to 1.9,** deactivate every Slack-format webhook
   endpoint: 1.9 sends it the Kiln envelope, which Slack refuses.
5. **Webhook endpoints that should carry the new `security.blocked_ip` event**
   need it ticked. Until then, a site whose alerts reach admins only by
   webhook shows **Review** on "Security alerts reach someone".
6. **Calendars:** yearly and monthly repeat rules that use `BYMONTH`,
   `BYMONTHDAY` or `BYDAY` now expand as RFC 5545 says, which is what
   subscribed calendars already showed. An event page may list different
   dates than it did in 1.9.
7. **API clients** that open `/ws/gql` with an expired token or an API key
   now get a refused connect (see Breaking changes).

No setting changes behavior on upgrade except those above. Requiring two-step
sign-in does nothing until a site admin saves it.

## New features

### Require two-step sign-in

A site admin can require a second factor (an authenticator app or a passkey)
of **Administrators**, **Editors and administrators**, or **Everyone**. A
grace period of 7, 14, 30 or 90 days gives people time to set one up. Past
it, an account without a second factor is held: its browser pages go to the
setup page, and its JWTs get `403 second_factor_required`. API keys are
exempt.

**In the console:** **Organization** → **Two-step sign-in**
(`/editor/two-factor`, site admins), choose
**Require two-step sign-in for** and a **Grace period**, and save. The page
first previews how many people would be asked to set it up, and how many of
them would be held at once. After saving, it shows per role who is covered,
who has a factor, who is still in grace and who is held. Saving needs your
own second factor. Security posture gets a **Two-step sign-in required** line
that reads Review while someone is in their grace period.

People set their factor up at `/account/two-factor` on the main or console
host. Giving someone a covered role, or making them a platform admin, warns
when they have no second factor yet.

**If the requirement locks everyone out**, an operator can turn it off from
a release console:

```bash
bin/kiln_cms rpc 'KilnCMS.Release.second_factor_requirement_off()'
bin/kiln_cms rpc 'KilnCMS.Release.second_factor_requirement_off(org: "acme")'
```

It returns `{:ok, :off}` and records the change in the site's security log.

Read more: [Two-factor authentication](../two-factor-auth.md).

### Backup schedules

Kiln can now take its own backups on a schedule, as an alternative to the
host's cron job. A schedule backs up the database, the media or both, daily,
weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly, at a local time in a time zone. One node
takes each backup, however many nodes run.

**In the console:** **Backups** → **Schedule** (`/editor/backups/schedules`,
platform admins only) → **Add a schedule**. The preview shows the cadence
and the first backup as you type. Under **How long to keep its backups**,
**Keep the newest** keeps that many of the schedule's backups however old
(1 to 1000), and **Keep for (days)** keeps them longer or shorter than
`BACKUP_KEEP_DAYS` (1 to 3650). Each card has **Edit**, **Pause**/**Resume**
and **Delete**. Scheduled runs read **Scheduled** in History.

**From a release console** (the shape is copied from
`test/kiln_cms/backups/schedule_policies_test.exs`):

```bash
bin/kiln_cms rpc '
admin = KilnCMS.Accounts.get_user_by_email!("<you@example.com>", authorize?: false)
{:ok, s} = KilnCMS.Backups.create_backup_schedule(%{components: [:db], interval: :daily,
  time_of_day: ~T[03:00:00], starts_on: Date.utc_today(), time_zone: "Europe/Paris"}, actor: admin)
IO.inspect(s.id)'
```

`update_backup_schedule/3` takes `keep_count` and `keep_days`, and pauses a
schedule with `enabled: false`. Schedules more often than daily are refused.

Read more: [Backups](../backups.md).

### Limit what a reference field can pick, and create from the field

A reference (relationship) field can now say which records it accepts: by
state, "only records the editor created", and per target type by tags,
category and custom-field values. The picker lists only those records, a
footer names the conditions, and every write path (editor, JSON:API, GraphQL,
MCP, import) refuses anything else. A field can also let editors create what
they type: a name that is not found offers *Create “name” as a new Page*,
made as a draft and connected at once.

**In the fields admin:** edit a relationship field, then use the steps
**Which records can be picked** and **Let editors create what they type**.
A sentence reads the setting back.

**In a seed or console** (the shape is copied from
`test/kiln_cms/cms/reference_selection_test.exs`):

```elixir
CMS.update_field_definition!(
  field,
  %{
    selection: %{
      "states" => ["published"],
      "owner" => "self",
      "sort" => "title",
      "by_type" => %{
        "page" => %{"tags_any" => [tag_id], "category" => category_id}
      }
    }
  },
  actor: admin
)
```

A record already connected is never judged again. `GET /api/schema` reports
the setting as `x-kiln-selection`. The API never creates a record from a
title: creating from the field is an editor shortcut.

Read more: [Extending content → Limit what can be picked](../extending-content.md#limit-what-can-be-picked).

### Security alerts in a chat channel

A webhook endpoint can now be Slack-format. It posts the site's security
alerts into a channel as a readable message, and Mattermost, Rocket.Chat and
Discord's `/slack` URL take the same body. New alert kinds come with it: a
request refused by the block list raises `blocked_ip` (naming the rule,
never the address), and platform admins hear when a verified API key goes
over a rate limit (`api_key_rate_limited`).

**In the console:** `/editor/webhooks` → **Add a webhook** → **Format** →
**Slack (incoming webhook)**, and paste the channel's incoming-webhook URL.
The event list narrows to the `security` group. A Slack endpoint has no
signing secret, because its URL is the credential. Webhook URLs are now
masked everywhere; **Reveal URL** shows one.

Security posture gets a **Security alerts reach someone** line. It reads
Review when the site's alerts are off or would reach no admin and no
webhook.

Read more: [Security alerts → Send alerts to Slack](../security-alerts.md#send-alerts-to-slack).

### Also new

- The editor's image picker has its own **Upload** button and drop zone; the
  uploaded image is used right away.
  ([#2791](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/pull/2791))
- At the two-step code prompt, an account that also has a passkey can choose
  **Use a passkey** instead (on the main or console host).
  ([#2825](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/pull/2825))
- Account owners are emailed when an authenticator app is set up or replaced,
  or a passkey is added.
  ([#2825](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/pull/2825))
- Search-engine claims are checked against the crawlers' published IP lists
  before DNS, so DuckDuckBot can be verified. New `KILN_CRAWLER_RANGES` and
  `KILN_CRAWLER_RANGES_CRON`.
  ([#2851](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/pull/2851))
- A Prometheus counter, `kiln_cms_api_invalid_bearer_count`, counts stale
  bearer tokens (needs `KILN_METRICS_ENABLED`).
  ([#2907](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/pull/2907))
- A new guide, [Take content down](../take-content-down.md), covers
  unpublishing and archiving from every place it can be done.

## Changes

- **Passkeys and the authenticator app are managed on the main and console
  hosts only.** Before, `/editor/settings` on a site's own address offered
  them. Now it links to the main host, and a passkey is pinned to the host
  that registered it. ([guide](../passkeys.md))
- **An account holds at most 20 passkeys and adds at most 5 an hour.**
  `/account/two-factor` lists your passkeys with a button to remove one.
  ([guide](../passkeys.md))
- **Removing a passkey asks for a second factor and emails the owner,** like
  turning off the authenticator app. ([guide](../two-factor-auth.md))
- **Authenticator-app secrets are stored encrypted** with `SECRET_KEY_BASE`,
  so a database dump without it carries no usable second factor.
- **Webhook URLs are masked** (`https://host/…/last4`) on the Webhooks page,
  in the governance trail and its exports, and in logs. The edit form starts
  blank, and leaving it blank keeps the URL. ([guide](../webhooks.md))
- **Select all in a webhook's event list no longer ticks `security.*`
  events;** the `security` group's own Select all still does.
- **No form field can be named `website`,** the spam trap's input. A field
  that already has that name keeps working and is flagged in the builder.
  ([guide](../forms.md))
- **`backup.sh` keeps a live run's `.partial` files fresh, and gives the
  database password to `pg_dump` as `PGPASSWORD`,** never on the command
  line. A run killed outright shows as Interrupted after 3 quiet hours.
  ([guide](../backups.md))
- **Backups History checks each artifact and media part on its own,** and
  says "couldn't read" instead of "none yet" when the backup directory
  can't be listed. ([guide](../backups.md))
- **Node moves to 22.23.5** for building assets. A project that builds Kiln
  from source moves its own `.tool-versions` to match.

## Breaking changes

### `/ws/gql` refuses a token it cannot verify

**Who is affected:** clients that open the GraphQL websocket with a stored
JWT after it expires, or with a `kiln_` API key, which this socket has never
accepted. Before, such a connect read published content as anonymous. Now
the handshake answers `403`. A connect with no token is unchanged, and
`/api/*` and `/gql` have answered such a token `401` since 1.9. Each refused
connect is counted in `kiln_cms_api_invalid_bearer_count`
(`surface="graphql"`).

**Before:**

```js
// A stale token silently read as the public.
const socket = new Socket("wss://<your-site>/ws/gql", { params: { token: storedToken } })
```

**After:**

```js
// Send a token only while it is valid; refresh it first, or connect without one.
const params = tokenIsValid(storedToken) ? { token: storedToken } : {}
const socket = new Socket("wss://<your-site>/ws/gql", { params })
```

With an API key, use `POST /gql` instead. `KILN_API_REJECT_INVALID_BEARER=false`
restores the anonymous connect while that escape hatch lasts.

### Media purge and restore in overlay code

**Who is affected:** overlays and plugins that call `purge_media_item` or
`restore_media_item` directly. The API never exposed either.

`CMS.purge_media_item` takes only a trashed item, and the media system actor
can no longer run it. The pipeline's purge of a quarantined upload is the new
`CMS.purge_quarantined_media_item`, which only `KilnCMS.Media.system/0` may
run. `CMS.restore_media_item` returns `StaleRecord` for an item no longer in
the trash.

**Before:**

```elixir
CMS.purge_media_item(item, actor: KilnCMS.Media.system())
```

**After:**

```elixir
CMS.purge_quarantined_media_item(item, actor: KilnCMS.Media.system())
```

Read more: [Trash and restore → For developers](../trash.md#for-developers).

### Custom-field requests that are now a `400`

**Who is affected:** API clients that relied on a few requests the server
used to read in a way the client was unlikely to mean. Each now answers
`400` with a message naming the field or operator:

| Request | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| `[]` for a single custom field | cleared it | `400`; send `null` or `""` to clear |
| a list beside `ilike` | joined into one pattern | `400` |
| a list beside `eq` / `not_eq` on a media or reference field | joined into one id | `400`; use `in` |
| a `null` `ilike` operand | an empty pattern | `400` |
| a `{year, month, day}` object as a date operand | read as that date | `400`; send `2026-03-01` |

**Before:**

```bash
curl -s "https://<your-site>/api/content/<type>?custom_filter[cover][eq][]=<media-id>"
```

**After:**

```bash
curl -s "https://<your-site>/api/content/<type>?custom_filter[cover][in][]=<media-id>"
```

`not_eq` on a single string, URL, select, email, color, telephone, time or
slug field now also leaves out the value as a save stores it, so
`custom_filter[accent][not_eq]=#FFF` drops a record holding `#ffffff`.

Read more: [JSON:API → Custom fields](../json-api.md).

## Deprecations

- `KilnCMS.Backups.Worker`, `KilnCMS.Backups.enqueue/1` and the `backups`
  Oban queue are still in 1.10, although the 1.9.0 changelog said this minor
  would remove them. They stay deprecated: call `KilnCMS.Backups.request/2`
  instead, and drain the `backups` queue before upgrading to the release that
  removes them, which its changelog will name.
- `KILN_API_REJECT_INVALID_BEARER=false` still restores the anonymous
  fallback. It is removed once `kiln_cms_api_invalid_bearer_count` shows that
  stale tokens have stopped arriving.

## Known issues

- **Backups History shows an "ok" off-site copy for a cron backup that
  failed.** Affects no release: only an install that deployed an
  unreleased `main` build between 2026-11-07 and 2026-11-08 and imported a
  cron failure then. On such an install, treat those rows as never copied
  off-site. No repair is planned.
  ([#2485](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2485))
- **GraphQL `customWhere` with `dateTime` can miss date-time list items saved
  with fractional seconds.** Not reproduced yet. Filter with `custom_filter`
  on the REST API instead.
  ([#2678](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2678))
- **Renaming a field's section can move or leave its layout wrongly when
  another admin changed the same type meanwhile.** Reload Fields before
  renaming a section, and check the type's sections afterwards.
  ([#2652](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2652))
- **On a site other than the default one, New draft and Translations can be
  offered or hidden by the default site's type scope.** Saving is still
  checked against the right site. No workaround.
  ([#2599](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2599))
- **Lock and teaser pages, embedded forms and newsletter confirmation mail
  can be in the wrong language on a multilingual site.** No workaround yet.
  ([#2597](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2597))
- **A gallery carousel can't be scrolled with the keyboard.** Affects
  keyboard and switch users. Use the grid layout where that matters.
  ([#2590](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2590))
- **The sign-in page on a site's own address offers "Use a passkey", which
  fails there.** Passkeys work only on the main and console hosts. Sign in
  there, or with a password and code on the site's address.
  ([#2827](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2827))
- **"Confirm it's you with your passkey" shows no passkey button.** Affects
  passkey-only accounts in a browser without WebAuthn or with JavaScript off.
  Use another browser, a recovery code, or ask an admin.
  ([#2887](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2887))
- **A quarantined upload released while the quarantine reaper sweeps can stay
  in the library without its original file.** Rare. Upload the file again and
  delete the broken item.
  ([#2835](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2835))
- **A recurrence rule with a date-time `UNTIL` (`…T000000Z`) can end a day
  early or late.** Affects events whose zone is not UTC. Write `UNTIL` as a
  date (`UNTIL=20261231`).
  ([#2861](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2861))
- **A member who starts checkout again for the tier they hold drops to
  "incomplete" and loses access.** Affects paid memberships, comps included.
  The provider's next subscription update restores a paid one; comp a comped
  one again.
  ([#2747](https://github.com/The-Verscienta/kiln_cms/issues/2747))
